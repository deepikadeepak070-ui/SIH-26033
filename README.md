## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
Our proposed solution is a smart digital platform that creates a direct connection between farmers, Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs), retailers, and consumers.
- Smart Product Listing: Farmers can upload their products with details such as quantity, quality, price, and availability.
- Direct Buyer Connection: Consumers, retailers, and bulk buyers can discover agricultural products without depending on multiple intermediaries.
- Intelligent Price Suggestions: The platform provides estimated market prices to help farmers make better selling decisions.
- Order Management: Buyers can place orders, compare products, and track their purchases through a single platform.
- Smart Logistics Coordination: The system connects available transportation services with farmers and buyers for efficient delivery.
Innovation: Unlike ordinary online marketplaces, our platform combines agricultural trading, intelligent pricing, and logistics coordination in one place.

## Technical Approach
The project follows a modular development approach where each feature works together to provide a smooth marketplace experience.
- Frontend Development: Create a simple and user-friendly interface for farmers, buyers, and delivery partners.
- Backend Development: Develop a centralized system to manage user accounts, product listings, orders, and transactions.
- Database Management: Store farmer details, product information, buyer requirements, order history, and delivery information.
- AI Integration: Apply machine learning techniques to estimate product demand and identify suitable selling opportunities.
- Route Optimization: Use location-based services to identify efficient delivery routes and reduce unnecessary transportation.
- Testing and Deployment: Test the platform with sample agricultural data before making it available to users.

## Feasibility and Viability
Remove These Lines
The proposed platform is practical because it can be developed using commonly available digital technologies and expanded gradually.
- Technical Feasibility: Web technologies, cloud databases, mapping services, and machine learning tools can support the platform.
- Economic Feasibility: Starting with a small group of farmers and buyers reduces initial development and operational expenses.
- Operational Feasibility: A simple interface with regional language support can make the platform easier for farmers to use.
- Scalability: The system can initially serve one agricultural region and later expand to multiple districts and states.
- Risk Management: Product quality differences, delivery delays, and inaccurate demand predictions can be addressed through verification, order tracking, and continuous data updates.
Long-Term Viability: The platform can generate revenue through optional premium services, logistics partnerships, and bulk-order management services.
## Impact and Benefits
The platform aims to create a more transparent, connected, and efficient agricultural trading ecosystem.
- For Farmers: Provides wider market access, improved price visibility, and opportunities to reach more buyers.
- For Consumers: Makes agricultural products more accessible and provides greater transparency in pricing and sourcing.
- For Retailers: Simplifies bulk purchasing and helps identify suppliers based on product availability.
- For Transport Providers: Creates opportunities to receive delivery requests and improve vehicle utilization.
- For the Environment: Better route planning and improved demand estimation can help reduce unnecessary transportation and food wastage.
- For Rural Development: Encourages digital participation and supports the growth of technology-enabled agricultural businesses.
